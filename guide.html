How to Use CarCare

01   Add your vehicle

Enter year, make, model, and current mileage.

02   Record maintenance

Add completed services, dates, costs, and mileage.

03   Review reminders

Check upcoming maintenance based on your entries.

04   Track spending

Review vehicle-related service and repair expenses.

Prototype note: Maintenance intervals are estimates. Follow your vehicle manufacturer’s guidance.

In the current interactive demo, enter a service name, due mileage, and cost. Full service-date and repair-history functionality is planned for a later version.
